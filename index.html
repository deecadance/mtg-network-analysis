Each archetype as an onion: core cards in the centre, support and periphery cards further out. Bombs, strong on their own, sit in a separate island unless they are core or support cards.

How Do I Use This Website?
1. Pick a set, then an archetype The set page lists its archetypes, largest first, with deck share, win rate, core cards and bombs.
2. Start in the Core view Use it to answer “what is this deck built from?” Core cards sit in the centre, support and periphery cards further out. The island beside the rings holds bombs: strong on their own, often worth a splash.
3. Check the Synergy view Use it to answer “which pairs win more together?” Only pairs that pass the statistical test are drawn. Many archetypes have few: their cards mostly win on their own.
4. Draft with the Picks view Use it to answer “what should I take for this deck?” Bigger cards raise draft win rate more; cards that do not are faded.
5. Click a card to learn more about it The spotlight shows its win rate in hand, verified synergy partners, the cards it is played with most, pick score, ALSA, and wheel chance.
6. Thinking of a splash? Open the Splash guide It compares decks that splashed each colour with decks of the same colours that did not, and lists the fixing that set offers.
